## Step 1 — Open outer shell

**Tools required:** Soft cloth, Torx T10 screwdriver, Phillips PH1 screwdriver, Plastic pry tool, Hands

1. Place the machine upside-down on a soft cloth so the bottom plate is accessible; remove any residual water from the tank seat to avoid leaks.
2. Unscrew the 4 Torx T10 screws located at the corners of the bottom plate.
3. Unscrew the 2 Phillips PH1 screws behind the rear panel, near the power-cord grommet.
4. Release the 4 internal plastic clips along the vertical edges by gently prying with a flat plastic tool.
5. Lift the upper shell vertically, keeping it parallel to the base, to separate it from the internal chassis.

### Parts obtained

- **Outer shell** (260 g, ABS (50% PCR on Responsible variant))

➡️ Continue disassembling **Chassis (full)** in [Step 2](#step-2)

<a id="step-2"></a>
## Step 2 — Remove user parts

**Tools required:** Hands, Phillips PH1 screwdriver

1. Lift out the 0.5 L removable water tank from its seat at the rear of the machine.
2. Pull out the used-capsule drawer from the front-bottom slot.
3. Release the 2 Phillips PH1 screws of the capsule lever / brewing-group assembly and lift it out.

### Parts obtained

- **Water tank** (150 g, SAN / transparent ABS + silicone valve)
- **Capsule drawer** (60 g, ABS + stainless grid)
- **Capsule lever** (130 g, Glass-filled nylon + stainless piercing plate + silicone seals)

➡️ Continue disassembling **Chassis (no user parts)** in [Step 3](#step-3)

<a id="step-3"></a>
## Step 3 — Remove control panel

**Tools required:** JST connector tool, Phillips PH0 screwdriver, Hands

1. Unplug the JST-XH 4-pin connector that links the front button board to the main PCB; press the latch before pulling.
2. Unplug the JST-PH 2-pin connector of the aluminium-capsule sensor, located just behind the capsule lever assembly.
3. Unscrew the 2 Phillips PH0 screws that hold the button panel to the front of the chassis.
4. Slide the button panel forward and lift it out together with the integrated LED indicators and the capsule sensor.

### Parts obtained

- **Button panel** (35 g, FR-4 PCB + tactile switches + LEDs + ABS cover)
- **Capsule sensor** (18 g, Copper coil + ferrite core + plastic housing)

➡️ Continue disassembling **Chassis (no panel)** in [Step 4](#step-4)

<a id="step-4"></a>
## Step 4 — Extract hydraulic circuit

**Tools required:** Container, Hose clamp pliers, Hands, Phillips PH1 screwdriver

1. Place a container under the chassis to catch ~30 mL of residual water that will drip from the tubing during removal.
2. Release the spring clamp on the silicone tube from the tank valve to the pump inlet; slide the tube off the barb.
3. Release the spring clamps on the silicone tubes from the pump outlet to the flow meter, and from the flow meter to the thermoblock inlet.
4. Unplug the 2-pin power connector that supplies the pump from the main PCB.
5. Unscrew the 2 Phillips PH1 screws holding the pump bracket to the chassis, then lift the pump together with the flow meter and the tubing as a single hydraulic assembly.

### Parts obtained

- **Vibration pump** (185 g, Steel body, copper winding, brass fittings, rubber dampers)
- **Silicone tubing** (22 g, Food-grade silicone + zinc-plated steel spring clamps)
- **Flow meter** (28 g, ABS housing + ceramic shaft + Hall sensor PCB)

➡️ Continue disassembling **Chassis (no hydraulic)** in [Step 5](#step-5)

<a id="step-5"></a>
## Step 5 — Extract PCB & wiring

**Tools required:** JST connector tool, Phillips PH0 screwdriver, Side cutters, Hands

1. Unplug all remaining JST connectors from the main PCB: thermostat lead, thermoblock heater lead, pump triac lead.
2. Disconnect the mains input wires from the PCB screw terminals (LIVE / NEUTRAL / EARTH).
3. Cut any cable ties that fix the wiring harness to the chassis ribs.
4. Unscrew the 4 Phillips PH0 screws securing the PCB to its plastic tray and lift the board out.
5. Pull the wiring harness and the power cord through the chassis cut-out as a single bundle.

### Parts obtained

- **Main PCB** (55 g, FR-4 PCB + SMD components + electrolytic capacitors + triacs)
- **Wiring harness** (70 g, Copper conductors + PVC insulation + nylon JST housings)
- **Power cord** (140 g, 3-core copper cable + PVC jacket + thermoplastic Schuko plug)

➡️ Continue disassembling **Chassis (only thermoblock)** in [Step 6](#step-6)

<a id="step-6"></a>
## Step 6 — Extract thermoblock

**Tools required:** Heat-resistant gloves (precaution), Hands, 10 mm wrench, Phillips PH1 screwdriver

1. Verify the machine has been unplugged for at least 30 minutes so the thermoblock has fully cooled before touching the metal body.
2. Disconnect the two faston terminals of the heating element from the harness, and the 2-pin JST of the safety thermostat.
3. Unscrew the brass nut at the thermoblock outlet (towards the brew chamber) using a 10 mm wrench; keep a cloth nearby for residual drips.
4. Unscrew the 2 Phillips PH1 screws of the thermoblock bracket on the chassis side wall.
5. Lift the thermoblock assembly vertically out of its seat.

### Parts obtained

- **Chassis frame** (967 g, Galvanized steel + ABS brackets + EPDM feet)

➡️ Continue disassembling **Thermoblock assembly** in [Step 7](#step-7)

<a id="step-7"></a>
## Step 7 — Disassemble thermoblock

**Tools required:** Bench vice with soft jaws, Hex key (typically 4 mm), Soft mallet, Press / saw (destructive option)

1. Clamp the aluminium body in a bench vice with soft jaws to avoid marking the surface; keep the heating-element side accessible.
2. Unscrew the safety thermostat from its threaded seat on the body; it comes out as a single threaded brass capsule.
3. Push out the heating element from its dedicated tunnel inside the aluminium body, gently tapping with a soft mallet if needed.
4. Separate the stainless-steel tube (water path) from the aluminium body; depending on the design, this may require a press or destructive cut.

### Parts obtained

- **Thermoblock fasteners** (20 g, Zinc-plated steel screws + silicone/graphite gaskets)
- **Heating element** (45 g, Nichrome (NiCr 80/20) wire + ceramic carrier + stainless sheath)
- **Safety thermostat** (15 g, Brass body + bimetal disc + ceramic insulator)
- **Aluminium body** (240 g, Cast aluminium alloy (AlSi9Cu3 or similar))
- **Stainless tube** (60 g, Stainless steel AISI 304)
